04

How cover changes with age

Protection is not static. Two of Jordan's term plans fall away at ages 70 and 85, and the daughters' multipliers expire at 80. Nothing lapses in the next decade — these are long-horizon transitions, mapped here so they never surprise.

Jordan Lee Wei Han · age 30 → 100

At 70, Elite Term (II) — the S$1m backbone — matures. At 80 the 5× multiplier expires, stepping death, TPD and critical illness toward the base. At 85, Family Care ends. A whole-of-life base of S$50,000 (LifeReady 20) then continues for life. Critical-illness cover always sits at or above early-CI — early-CI is the early-stage subset of it, not a separate layer.

Each daughter · to age 100

Both daughters carry the same shape of protection: S$250,000 across death, disability and both critical-illness covers until age 80, then S$50,000 base cover for life — Emma through LifeReady Plus (II), Olivia through Complete Life Secure.

07

Notes & actions

Seven things worth attending to, in rough order of importance.

Critical illness now meets the benchmark. Read as a whole — early CI being the subset within it — total CI cover is S$562,000 against the S$548,000 benchmark, a S$14,000 surplus. The late-stage portion alone is S$312,000. Data is kept per illustration, with CI and early-CI in separate columns.

Watch the joint account. S$34,949 of its S$36,000 yearly top-up is already committed — 97% utilised, S$1,051 of slack. One more joint-funded policy would tip it past capacity.

Premiums exceed income by design — keep it deliberate. Total premiums equal 102% of yearly income, with wealth plans at 87%. Sustainable only while the joint-account contribution holds; revisit if circumstances change.

Obtain the Manulink illustration. Three of four ILPs have projected maturity values on file; Manulink Investor (CPF-OA) does not, so its share of the wealth picture can't yet be measured.

Nominate beneficiaries. Only LifeReady 20 records nominations (Lee Wei Kang 50%, Emma 50%). The rest of the register has none documented.

Complete Elite Term's record. The S$1m Elite Term (II) has no policy number yet — to be captured at the next meeting. Source is now set to Self.

Diary the long-horizon cliffs. Elite Term (II) ends at age 70 and the daughters' 5× multipliers at 80 — far off, but each is a six-figure drop in cover worth a review when nearer. Emma's Child Care Benefit also lapses at her age 25 (2048).
